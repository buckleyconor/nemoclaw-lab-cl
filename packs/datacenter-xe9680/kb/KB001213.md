# GPU Thermal Throttle on XE9680 — Detection, Cooling Check, and Resolution

> **PLACEHOLDER** — SME: replace body with the real Dell KB article for thermal throttle on XE9680 / H100.

## Summary

The H100 SXM5 GPU activates thermal throttling when its junction temperature exceeds 85 °C. Clock speeds are automatically reduced to protect the hardware. Performance drops significantly (20–40%) and training jobs slow down, but no data is lost. The immediate goal is to restore thermal headroom.

## Affected products

- Dell PowerEdge XE9680 with NVIDIA H100 SXM5 80 GB GPUs
- Typical in high-density racks or data centres with elevated ambient temperatures

## Symptoms

- iDRAC Lifecycle Log shows `OEM.1.0.ThermalThrottle` with severity Warning
- `nvidia-smi` shows clock throttle reason: `HW Thermal Slowdown`
- DCGM: `GPU temperature threshold exceeded`, GPU SM clock below rated frequency
- Training throughput drops and job ETA increases
- iDRAC fan zones at or near 100%

## Root cause

Common causes:

1. Elevated inlet air temperature (data centre CRAC/CRAH issue)
2. Blocked hot-aisle containment or poor rack airflow
3. Overloaded workload driving sustained 400 W GPU power draw
4. Failed or degraded fan in the chassis

## Remediation steps

### Step 1 — Reduce GPU workload to lower temperature

Temporarily cap GPU power to allow temperature to fall:

```bash
nvidia-smi -pl 300 -i <gpu_index>   # reduce from 400 W to 300 W
```

Monitor temperature recovery:

```bash
watch -n 2 nvidia-smi --query-gpu=temperature.gpu,clocks_throttle_reasons.hw_thermal_slowdown --format=csv
```

Wait for temperature to drop below 80 °C and confirm throttle reason clears.

### Step 2 — Check cooling infrastructure

1. Verify inlet temperature in iDRAC (target ≤ 25 °C): `racadm getsensorinfo | grep Inlet`
2. Check fan speeds: `racadm getsensorinfo | grep Fan` — all fans should be present and spinning
3. Inspect hot-aisle containment for bypass airflow
4. If a fan has failed, replace immediately (hot-swap capable)

### Step 3 — Confirm GPU temperature below threshold after cooling

```bash
nvidia-smi --query-gpu=temperature.gpu,clocks_throttle_reasons.hw_thermal_slowdown --format=csv,noheader
```

All GPUs should read below 80 °C and throttle reason `0`. Restore power limit:

```bash
nvidia-smi -pl 400 -i <gpu_index>
```

Monitor for 10 minutes to confirm stability before returning to full workload.

## Escalation

If inlet temperature is within spec (≤ 25 °C) and fans are healthy but throttle persists, the GPU's heat spreader or liquid cooling loop may require Dell service inspection.
