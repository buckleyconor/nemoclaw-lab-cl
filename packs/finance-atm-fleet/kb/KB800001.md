# Dispenser Transport Jam: Cash Handler Fatal Error on Lobby Series 84 ATMs

| | |
|---|---|
| **Article ID** | KB800001 |
| **Version** | 5 |
| **Last updated** | 2025-10-30 |
| **Classification** | Break/Fix — Cash Dispenser / Note Transport |
| **Default severity** | Critical (Sev 2 — withdrawal function down, deposits/inquiries may remain available) |
| **Audience** | ATM field service technicians, branch operations, cash management |
| **Applies to** | Lobby Series 84 ATM, NDC protocol devices |

## Summary

A dispenser transport jam occurs when a note fails to complete its path from the cassette through the pick, qualification, and transport mechanism to the exit slot, physically obstructing the transport. The NDC status code 68 ("cash handler fatal") marks the dispenser as fatally faulted and the ATM suspends withdrawal transactions immediately — deposits, balance inquiries, and transfers may remain available depending on configuration, since those functions don't route through the dispenser transport. The critical operational concern beyond the physical jam itself is reconciliation: if the jam occurs mid-transaction, the customer's account may have been debited for notes that never reached the exit slot, which the electronic journal (EJ) must reconcile against the physical note count found during recovery — this is a cash-accountability issue, not just a mechanical fault.

## Affected products

- Lobby Series 84 (and Lobby Series 34/23/27 family, same NDC transport architecture)
- NDC protocol status/error code framework

## Error signature

The canonical signatures for this fault are:

- `Dispenser transport jam`
- `notes jammed in transport`
- `cash handler fatal error`

NDC device log:

```
CRIT  ndc[atm-02]: Dispenser transport jam — notes jammed in transport, cash handler fatal error, withdrawals suspended
CRIT  ndc[atm-02]: Status code 68 — cash handler fatal, device out of service for withdrawal function
```

## Symptoms

- Note qualification reject rate elevated in the dispenses immediately preceding the jam — an early indicator when a marginal note-quality or sensor issue is building toward a full jam
- Note thickness/skew sensor flags during pick, followed by a pick retry, shortly before the fatal fault
- Status code 68 (cash handler fatal) logged, withdrawal function immediately suspended
- Customer-facing screen switches to "Temporarily Out of Service"
- EJ (electronic journal) flags the in-progress transaction as indeterminate, requiring reconciliation
- Card retained per fault-safe procedure if the jam occurs while a transaction (and therefore the card) was still in progress

## Sample log excerpt

Representative sequence from `atm-02` (Lobby Series 84, Main Branch Drive-Up) during normal withdrawal service:

```
11:14:51Z INFO  ndc[atm-02]: Transaction complete — withdrawal $100, cassette 1 (20s), dispense OK
11:26:07Z WARN  ndc[atm-02]: Note qualification reject rate elevated — cassette 2, 3 rejects in last 5 dispenses
11:31:44Z WARN  ndc[atm-02]: Note thickness/skew sensor flagged during pick — cassette 2, note pick retry
11:33:12Z CRIT  ndc[atm-02]: Dispenser transport jam — notes jammed in transport, cash handler fatal error, withdrawals suspended
11:33:12Z CRIT  ndc[atm-02]: Status code 68 — cash handler fatal, device out of service for withdrawal function
11:33:13Z CRIT  ndc[atm-02]: Transaction in progress at time of fault — customer card retained per fault-safe procedure
11:33:15Z WARN  ndc[atm-02]: EJ (electronic journal) flag — transaction outcome indeterminate, reconciliation required
```

The reject-rate warning on cassette 2 roughly 7 minutes before the fatal jam, and the note pick-retry flag 90 seconds before it, are the leading indicators — worth trending across the fleet to catch a degrading cassette before it produces a fatal jam.

## Impact

- **Workload impact:** The ATM cannot dispense cash from the moment the jam occurs until resolved. Deposit, balance inquiry, and transfer functions may remain available depending on the site's configuration, since those don't route through the dispenser transport.
- **Service risk:** Medium — beyond the mechanical fault, a jam mid-transaction creates a reconciliation exposure: the customer's account may show a debit for notes that never reached the exit slot. This must be resolved through EJ reconciliation against the physical note count found during recovery, not assumed resolved just because the transport is physically cleared.
- **Estimated remediation duration:** ~5 minutes for a remote reset attempt. If unsuccessful, field dispatch and physical clear/reconciliation is typically same-day but not immediate — plan for extended out-of-service time on this unit.

## Root cause

In observed order of frequency:

1. **Note quality degradation** — worn, folded, taped, or damaged notes in the cassette fail qualification sensors partway through the transport, jamming rather than being cleanly rejected to the reject bin. The elevated reject rate preceding the jam in this event is consistent with a cassette carrying degraded note stock.
2. **Transport belt or roller wear** — mechanical wear in the pick/transport mechanism causes notes to skew or double-feed, jamming even with good-quality notes.
3. **Sensor misalignment or contamination** — dust or debris on note-qualification sensors causes false skew/thickness readings, triggering retries that increase jam risk on marginal notes.
4. **Cassette loading error** — notes loaded with inconsistent orientation, over-stuffed, or with a foreign object (paperclip, damaged note edge) introduced during a cash replenishment.

## Remediation steps

### Step 1 — Issue remote dispenser reset/purge command (`remote_dispenser_reset`)

From the ATM monitoring/fleet management platform, attempt this before any physical dispatch — it clears a meaningful share of transport jams without a truck roll:

```
ATM monitoring platform: select ATM-02 → Dispenser → Reset/Purge
Confirm command acknowledged by the terminal
Wait for terminal to report dispenser status post-reset
```

### Step 2 — Run dispenser self-test (`run_self_test`)

```
ATM monitoring platform: select ATM-02 → Dispenser → Self-Test
Confirm transport path reports clear across all cassettes
Confirm no residual reject-bin overflow or sensor fault flags
```

If self-test passes cleanly, the ATM can be returned to service — but flag the cassette that showed elevated rejects (cassette 2 in this event) for physical inspection at the next scheduled replenishment, since a reset clearing the jam doesn't address degraded note stock still loaded.

### Step 3 — Set ATM out-of-service if reset does not clear the jam (`set_out_of_service`)

```
If self-test fails or reports persistent transport fault, confirm/maintain OUT_OF_SERVICE status
Do not attempt repeated remote resets beyond 2 attempts — repeated resets on a physically jammed transport risk compounding the mechanical fault
```

### Step 4 — Dispatch field engineer with cassette/EJ reconciliation task (`dispatch_field_engineer`)

```
Dispatch with explicit task: physical transport clear AND EJ reconciliation for any transaction flagged indeterminate at time of fault
Field engineer to count and log any notes recovered from the transport path against the EJ-flagged transaction amount
Do not close the reconciliation task on physical clear alone — EJ discrepancy must be resolved (either matched to recovered notes or escalated per cash-difference procedure) before the incident is closed
```

## Escalation

- Jam recurs on the same cassette within 7 days of a prior jam and reset → escalate to field service for cassette/transport mechanical inspection; a pattern this tight points at a hardware wear issue, not a one-off note-quality problem.
- EJ reconciliation cannot account for the note discrepancy after physical recovery → escalate to cash management / branch operations per the institution's cash-difference investigation procedure — do not resolve as a routine service ticket.
- Card retained during the fault and customer disputes the retention or a transaction amount → escalate to branch operations for customer service handling; this is separate from and in addition to the mechanical/reconciliation remediation above.
